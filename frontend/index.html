About · how to read this dashboard

Measured data

Energy and power summaries come from uploaded CSV readings or the clearly named public PVDAQ sample. Data completeness checks whether fields are present; it does not certify meter accuracy.

Forecast

The current forecast repeats a recent measured-power average. Its range is the historical minimum and maximum, not a calibrated prediction interval. Weather is fetched separately from Open-Meteo when the computer can reach it.

Investigations

Persistent gaps between measured power and a simple capacity/irradiance baseline are investigation signals only. They do not prove a fault, shading, or soiling.

Data and limitations

The public example is NREL PVDAQ system 1245, New Orleans, July 1–20, 2012, sampled at about five-minute intervals. It is old public research data, not a connection to your equipment. PVDAQ attribution and license: Data.gov dataset record · CC BY 4.0.

CSV upload is the implemented inverter-data integration. Vendor APIs, edge gateways and email/SMS notifications are not connected. Use “Save report as PDF” to print this view to a PDF file.
